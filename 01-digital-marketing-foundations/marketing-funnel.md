## 1. Beginner Explanation

Imagine you own a **website design business**.
Not everyone who sees your post will buy immediately. People move through stages.

### 🔵 TOFU (Top of Funnel) — Awareness
People discover you.

**Examples:**
- Facebook Post
- Instagram Reel
- Blog
- YouTube Video

**Goal:** Get attention.

### 🟡 MOFU (Middle of Funnel) — Consideration
People start trusting you.

**Examples:**
- Case Studies
- Free PDF
- Free Webinar
- Testimonials

**Goal:** Build trust.

### 🟢 BOFU (Bottom of Funnel) — Conversion
People buy.

**Examples:**
- Free Consultation
- Discount Offer
- Direct Call

**Goal:** Get customers.

### 🔻 Funnel Visualization

\`\`\`
      Awareness
          ↓
      Interest
          ↓
    Consideration
          ↓
       Decision
          ↓
       Purchase
          ↓
       Loyalty
\`\`\`

## 7. Interview Questions

**Q1: What is a Marketing Funnel?**
A marketing funnel is the journey a customer takes from discovering a brand to becoming a paying customer.

**Q2: What are the stages of a funnel?**
- Awareness
- Interest
- Consideration
- Decision
- Purchase

**Q3: Why is a funnel important?**
It helps businesses convert visitors into customers systematically.

**Q4: What is TOFU, MOFU, and BOFU?**
- **TOFU** = Awareness
- **MOFU** = Consideration
- **BOFU** = Conversion
