<a name="phase-1-topic-1--what-is-digital-marketing"></a>

## PHASE 1 TOPIC 1 — WHAT IS DIGITAL MARKETING?

### 🎯 Beginner Explanation

Digital Marketing means promoting products, services or brands through
digital channels such as search engines, websites, social media, email,
online advertising and content platforms.

### 🏢 Industry Use Case

Businesses use Digital Marketing to:

- Generate awareness
- Attract website visitors
- Generate leads
- Acquire customers
- Increase sales
- Build customer relationships
- Measure marketing performance

### 💼 Job Responsibilities

- Campaign planning
- Audience research
- Content planning
- SEO coordination
- Social media management
- Paid advertising
- Analytics
- Reporting

### 💰 Freelancing Opportunities

- Digital Marketing Management
- SEO Services
- Social Media Management
- Content Marketing
- Lead Generation
- Paid Ads Management

### 🧪 Practical Exercise

Choose a local business and create:

1. Target audience
2. Marketing funnel
3. Content plan
4. Lead generation strategy
5. Marketing KPIs

### 🚀 Real Project

**Local Business Digital Marketing Strategy**

Business → Audience → Offer → Content → Traffic → Leads → Conversion.

### 🎤 Interview Questions

- What is Digital Marketing?
- What are the major Digital Marketing channels?
- What is a marketing funnel?
- What is a conversion?
- What is ROI?

### ⚠️ Common Mistakes

- Focusing only on followers
- Ignoring customer intent
- Not tracking conversions
- Creating content without strategy
- Using every platform without purpose

### 🛠️ Recommended Free Tools

- Google Search
- Google Trends
- Google Sheets
- Google Analytics
- Canva

### 🔥 Advanced Concepts

- Full-funnel marketing
- Customer acquisition cost
- Customer lifetime value
- Attribution
- Marketing automation
- Omnichannel marketing
